Chapter I Written in the margins

Your record keeps the decisions. The margins keep the reasoning.

Insights sit scattered across inboxes, drives, and disconnected tools, leaving teams to make decisions without the full picture.

And when a committee chair or a long-serving colleague moves on, the reasoning in their margins goes with them.

Chapter II A layer above the record

Nothing underneath has to move.

“We sit above your existing systems to organize, connect, and activate relevant knowledge that no single tool can access on its own.”

  1. Organize what already exists, where it already lives.
  2. Connect across systems, and to the people behind them.
  3. Activate so the right insight reaches the right person at the right moment.

We plug into your existing AMS, CRM, LMS, and content systems. No rip and replace. Secure, private, and live in days — not months.2

Q. When our annual meeting went hybrid, how did we award continuing-education credit?

Credit went to live virtual attendance, verified at session check-in.1 The committee chose check-in over quizzes so hybrid attendees wouldn’t be penalized.2 Recordings watched later count only when a chapter confirms attendance,3 and staff apply the rule at renewal.4 Why exceptions were allowed was never written down.
