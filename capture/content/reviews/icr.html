Findings

Three things
the review found

Quoted from the full review. Each one carries its date, source and a fix inside.

01

“Tested, warranty” is in the title but nowhere in the listing

A buyer spending $3,500 on a pendant wants proof it works and to know what the warranty covers.

02

One robot family, seven spellings

Technicians search the way the nameplate or the manual reads, and plenty type it without the hyphen. A listing shouldn't depend on the search engine guessing that “R-2000iB” and “R2000iB” are the same robot.

03

Serial numbers use space that buyers’ search words should get

Nobody searches a serial number before they know it exists. Meanwhile the words buyers do type — “industrial robot arm,” “Yaskawa” for Motoman, payload, application — are missing.
