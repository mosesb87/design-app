Sec. 01 — Overview · for the Website Manager role

Found by people. Quoted by machines.

Salt is repositioning around agentic security and answer engines — but its pages give machines nothing to quote. 57 of 60 sampled pages carry no structured data. There's no FAQPage anywhere, even where the H2s are questions. llms.txt is a 404. And the sitemap submits 720 URLs that robots.txt disallows. Here's the answer layer I'd build in Webflow.
