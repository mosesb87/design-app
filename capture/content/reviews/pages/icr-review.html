What works

Start from what's already right

The store has the hard things in place: trust, a clean photo look, and real part numbers. The fixes below are about getting more buyers to find and believe each listing.

Trust

100% positive feedback across 2.3K items sold, 30-day returns with the seller paying return shipping, and 24-hour handling.

A consistent photo look

White sweep, the ICR mark in the corner, and a close-up of the nameplate — the label shot is exactly what a technician checks first.

Real identifiers

Titles lead with brand and model and carry the controller; parts carry the manufacturer part number and an MPN item specific.

Webstore structured data

Product pages publish Product schema with MPN, price, stock, RefurbishedCondition, and a 30-day return policy — a solid SEO foundation.

Local fulfillment

Listings ship from Warren (the pendant I checked quoted 2–4 day delivery), and the webstore offers same-day shipping on in-stock parts ordered by 1 PM.

12 findings

What I'd fix first

Everything below was captured from the live eBay store and icrservices.com on September 15, 2026. Each finding has the evidence, why it costs sales, and the fix. Fixes marked with a tool link are built further down this page.

01
High
eBay · item 132708949075

“Tested, warranty” is in the title but nowhere in the listing

ABB TPU2 teach pendant — US $3,499.95 or Best Offer Title: ABB TPU2-EX TEACH PENDANT 3HNE00442-1 **TESTED WARRANTY** Condition: Seller refurbished Description: weight, box size, payment and shipping terms — no test details, no warranty term, and all items are sold as used unless otherwise stated

A buyer spending $3,500 on a pendant wants proof it works and to know what the warranty covers. When the title, the condition, and the description say three different things, the buyer either messages and waits, or buys elsewhere — and a sale made on a mismatch can come back as “not as described.”

Fix

One description template for every tested item: what was tested and on which stand, the warranty length, and what's in the box — with the condition wording matching the title. See the rewritten listing →

02
High
eBay · FANUC titles

One robot family, seven spellings

R-2000 family across the 59 live titlesR2000IB · R2000iB · R-2000IB · R-2000IC · R2000IC · R2000iC · and once, FANUC's own R-2000iB (on a wrist listing) Controllers: R30IA · R30iA · R30IB · R30IB+ · R30IB Plus · R30IB PLUS — FANUC's own R-30iA / R-30iB Plus never appears

Technicians search the way the nameplate or the manual reads, and plenty type it without the hyphen. A listing shouldn't depend on the search engine guessing that “R-2000iB” and “R2000iB” are the same robot. Inconsistent naming also makes the store look like it was listed by five different people.

Fix

A naming convention: Brand + official model + type + official controller, then the no-hyphen variant where space allows (e.g. FANUC R-2000iB/210F Industrial Robot Arm w/ R-30iA Controller R2000iB 210F). Linter + rewrites →

03
Medium
eBay · 42 of 59 titles

Serial numbers are using space that buyers' search words should get

Longest example — 32 characters of an 80-character titleMOTOMAN UP6 ROBOT WITH XRC CONTROLLER SN: (R) S2M003-1-1 (C) S1M544-1-2

Nobody searches a serial number before they know it exists. Meanwhile the words buyers do type — “industrial robot arm,” “Yaskawa” for Motoman, payload, application — are missing. The serial already sits in the nameplate photo, which is the right place for it.

Fix

Move serials to an item specific and the label photo; spend the space on search terms. The UP6 becomes Yaskawa Motoman UP6 Industrial Robot Arm w/ XRC Controller UP-6 6-Axis (70/80).

04
Medium
eBay · 14 titles, 51 all-caps

Asterisks and all caps read as noise

Across the 59 titles**TESTED WARRANTY** ×10 · *REFURBISHED* ×4 · 51 of 59 titles written almost entirely in capitals

Symbols don't match any search and cost characters. “Refurbished” already exists as eBay's condition filter, so it earns its place in the title only as a plain word. Mixed case with correct model spelling (R-30iB, not R30IB) is easier to scan in a results page full of shouting titles.

Fix

Plain words (“Tested with Warranty”, “Refurbished”), brand-first, official capitalization. The linter below flags every case automatically.

05
High
eBay · items 167252727995, 167792818349, 168588511029

Part numbers disagree inside the same title

Allen-Bradley PowerFlex 700 — both spellings are in one titleALLEN BRADLEY POWERFLEX 700 125HP 20B C 170 A 0 AYANC0 20BC170A0AYNANC0 spaced version joined: 20BC170A0AY_ANC0 ≠ 20BC170A0AYNANC0 (one “N” missing) FANUC R-2000iB wrist — $14,249.95… WRIST A290-7329-T501**TESTED WARRANTY** (no space: the part number runs into the asterisks) FANUC R-2000iB/210F… W/ R30iA Controller R30iA SN: F-154664 (controller repeated)

Part-number buyers search the exact catalog string from their nameplate. One dropped character is a missed match — and a tech comparing the title to the drive in front of them will hesitate on a $9,499.95 purchase.

Fix

Copy part numbers from the label photo, not from memory, keep a space on both sides, and run a QA check before publishing. The linter flags repeated words, glued part numbers, and spaced-vs-joined part numbers that don't match.

Medium
eBay · item specifics

Item specifics stop at Brand, MPN, UPC

ABB TPU2 teach pendant — every item specific on the listingBrand: ABB · MPN: 3HNE00442-1 · UPC: Does not apply

Item specifics drive eBay's search filters and the “compatible with” questions buyers ask. With three fields, the listing can't show up when a buyer filters by type, model, or controller.

Fix

A specifics set per category (teach pendants, servo motors, drives, robots) filled from the label and the test record: Model, Type, Revision, Compatible Controller, Cable, Serial, Country of Manufacture, Warranty. Example →

Medium
eBay · photos

3 photos of 24 — and none of the pendant powered on

ABB TPU2 teach pendant — photo set1. front on sweep (display has glare) 2. three-quarter angle 3. nameplate missing: powered-on screen on the test stand · back · connector end · cable length · keypad/joystick close-ups · wear

For a tested electronic part, a photo of it booted on ICR's test stand is the most persuasive image in the listing — it proves the “tested” claim without a word. More angles also answer questions before buyers have to ask.

Fix

A 12-shot standard for parts, done the same way every time. Checklist →

The white sweep photographs light gray

Background sampled from photo 1 (median RGB)bottom-right 234, 238, 238 · bottom-left 236, 241, 243 · target 255, 255, 255

Next to competitors' pure-white thumbnails, a gray, slightly cool background looks dimmer in search results. It's a two-step fix that can be batched across every photo.

White-balance off the sweep, then set the white point — shadows kept, product untouched. Saved as a Photoshop action or a script so every set gets the same edit. Before/after →

Medium
eBay · label vs title

The nameplate says more than the title

Label in photo 3 vs the titleLabel: TPU2-EX · Art.no: 3HNE 00442-1/05 Title: 3HNE00442-1 (revision dropped; label's spaced form not included)

A technician replacing a failed unit often matches the full article number including the revision, and ABB prints it with a space — so both forms get typed into search.

Fix

Title carries 3HNE00442-1/05 and 3HNE 00442-1; revision goes into item specifics too.

Growth
eBay · assortment

The store is mostly whole robots — parts are the everyday buy

59 active listings48 complete robots ($6,999.95 – $36,080.95) 11 parts: 4 servo motors · 4 wrists · 1 teach pendant · 1 rectifier · 1 drive icrservices.com lists a refurbished FANUC P-155 wrist (EO-0833-010) IN STOCK at $8,995.95 — not on eBay; the ICR homepage cites 20,000 SKUs in its equipment catalogue

Maintenance teams buy pendants, motors, amplifiers, boards and cables every week; whole robots sell a few times a year. A robot that isn't moving can become a dozen listings once it's pulled and disassembled — which is exactly the work this role describes.

Fix

A part-out line for slow-moving robots, plus cross-listing in-stock refurbished parts from the webstore to eBay. Part-out planner →

11
Medium
icrservices.com · product page

Webstore pages lead with the part number only

/catalogue/fanuc-eo-0833-010<title> FANUC - EO-0833-010 | ICR (no product name) H1: FANUC - EO-0833-010 Meta description: P–155 Wrist Assembly (en dash; the Series field says P-155) Buy panel: Test Method · Test: (blank) · 1 product photo

Someone searching “FANUC P-155 wrist” doesn't see those words in the page title, and a typed hyphen doesn't match an en dash. The empty test field looks unfinished on a $9,000 item — the Product schema underneath is already excellent, so this is copy, not code.

Fix

Title: FANUC P-155 Wrist Assembly EO-0833-010, Refurbished | ICR. Description of ~150 characters with condition, test, warranty and same-day shipping. Fill the test method or hide the field when empty.

Store header100% positive · 2.3K items sold · 38 followers

Repeat industrial buyers are the best buyers, and followers see new listings. Almost none of the people who already bought have saved the store.

Fix

A packing-slip insert with a QR code to save the seller and a link to ICR's repair and buyback programs — cheap, and it turns one-time buyers into a channel.

The job

Bench-to-listing, every day

The posting lists the work plainly: prep, clean, pull and disassemble inventory, capture and edit images, create eBay and webstore listings, keep the area organized. Here's how I'd run that as one line, so nothing gets photographed twice or listed with the wrong part number.

Pull

Pull by bin location and tag the item before anything else happens to it.

tag: SKU · bin · date

Clean

Wipe down and degrease; the nameplate stays intact and readable.

no nameplate, no listing

Disassemble

For part-outs, remove sub-assemblies per plan; bag and tag each with the parent SKU.

child SKU → parent SKU

Test record

no record → “untested”

Photograph

12-shot standard, fixed lights and distance, label shot first.

set = one folder per SKU

Edit

Batch: white balance, white point, square crop, compress.

action / script, not by hand

List
