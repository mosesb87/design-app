Sec. 01 — Overview · for the Ecommerce Specialist role

Every number a shopper sees should be true.

The role is site conversion work — landing pages, PDPs, offers, bundles. So I read the product pages closely. The same PDP reports “3,302 reviews, 4.7” in one schema block and “8,848, 4.8” in another, no product shows a live rating, the footer's Reviews link redirects to the homepage, and “20% off every shipment” prices at 13.6–15% in the plans.
