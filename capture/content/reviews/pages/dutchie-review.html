Launch readiness · an outside read

Shipping is done. Ready is the other half.

A launch isn't finished when it ships; it's finished when every team is ready and every customer-facing surface says the same thing. So I read Dutchie's launches from the outside — release notes, monthly updates, help center, status page — and checked whether every surface told the same story on the same day.

L-01No-go

One feature, three states on three official surfaces

The Aug 27 release note says chain admins “now have a single Mobile App area”; the September monthly update leads with it as shipped. What's working

Release notes ship every Thursday, one POS and one E-Commerce entry, without fail — that's a real operating rhythm. Monthly updates have run every month of 2026. All 58 business-site nav links return 200. And the early-access pattern for Dutchie MCP — a help article live before GA — is exactly the pattern the other launches are missing. The gap isn't process; it's that no single check confirms every surface flipped together.

Method & scope

Public sources only, Sep 20, 2026: updates.dutchie.com (page 1, Aug 13–Sep 17), the monthly-update index, the help center's public search API and Product Announcements category, status.dutchie.com (incident titles from the page's embedded data) and the Aug 2025 Plus post. developers.dutchie.com didn't resolve for me, so absence of a Plus deprecation page is “not found publicly”, not proven absent. I didn't check social channels.
