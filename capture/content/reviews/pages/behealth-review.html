Sec. 01 — Overview · for the SEO Specialist role

Your own site tells Google it lives somewhere else.

Every page on behealthdigital.com — its canonical, its og:url, its sitemap entry and the Organization schema's @id — points to wellspring-web-make.lovable.app, a staging host that returns 404 “No published build.” The agency is telling search engines its real pages are duplicates of nothing. That's the first thing an SEO Specialist would fix — then run the same audit on every client.
