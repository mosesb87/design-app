Why this belongs on a launch checklist

On launch day, a readiness pass would already have caught four of these: the CT drawing date, the missing KY rules block, the MA typos and the IL eligibility wording. The fifth — the expiry — only shows up if someone owns the “take it down” date as a task. In the Deals Operating System that's C10 (dates valid, with an end-date owner) and C16 (wording passes policy), run per state version, not once per campaign.
