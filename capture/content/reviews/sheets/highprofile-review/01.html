What's working

State compliance lines are present and correctly different per market — Michigan's Poison Control line, Missouri's “Medical decisions should not be made based on advertising”, Massachusetts' CCC language, New Jersey's licensing line. That's the hard part of multi-state promo copy, and it's done. Regular prices on the house brands are consistent across the three Missouri stores for 204 identical SKUs; the drift is in promo depth, which is a rollout decision, not a data error. And every Missouri product page server-renders its full price and special data, which is what made this review checkable at all.

Method & scope

Public pages only, Sep 20, 2026: the seven state deals pages, 35 location pages, sitemaps, c3industries.com, and product pages for St Louis, Chesterfield and Branson (Missouri) plus Kalamazoo, Webberville, Buchanan, Grand Rapids 44th, Dorchester and Manchester menus via the site's own menu endpoint. Specials carry no start/end dates in the menu data, so expiry on menus couldn't be checked — only the WordPress deals pages expose dates. Cross-state price differences (MA vs MO) are expected and not counted. I haven't checked a cart.

What I bring, plainly. Six years of promotion calendars, catalog pricing and campaign pages for two Michigan wholesalers, and the QA system this review runs on. I haven't bought out-of-home or print media; this review is about digital rollout readiness, and that's the work I'm asking to do.
