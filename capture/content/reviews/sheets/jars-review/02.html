Why this matters for revenue

Store name, phone, hours and address are what Google Maps, Apple Maps and every “dispensary near me” search show before anyone reaches the menu. When the page says one thing and the data says another, search engines pick one — and a transposed phone number means the call goes somewhere else. One build rule fixes all of it: render every public field from the single store record, never type it twice.
