01

Make the quote step clear.

What I observed
The Vantage and Stryker S3 comparison shows $0.00 and Add To Cart. Their product pages say Call for Price and Add To Quote; the comparison action opens a quick view with Add To Quote.
Why it matters
A customer could expect a zero-price purchase before encountering a quote step. The labels disagree; their effect on behavior still needs testing.
What I would do
I would confirm the pricing status and use the same approved label and action across cards, comparison, quick view and product details.
How I would check
Repeat the same task with both quote products and a separately checked priced product. Verify labels, actions and configuration context on each surface.

02

Make filtering agree with the product facts.

What I observed
In the September 9 capture, Hospital Beds lists 21 products, including Stryker S3 With Scale. Selecting “500 lbs” leaves six results and removes that bed. Its product page lists “Weight Capacity: 500 lb (227 kg).”
Why it matters
A shopper using this filter could miss a bed whose own page lists the selected capacity. The page separately lists the bed’s own weight as 570 lb; that is not its weight capacity. I have not inspected the system behind the mismatch.
What I would do
I would ask the catalog owner to confirm the exact model and its approved capacity, then make the product-page value and the value used by the filter agree. I would preserve the existing filters and Clear All control.
How I would check
After confirmation, select “500 lbs” again and check that Stryker appears, other results remain appropriate, and refresh and Clear All work. The local demonstration uses a different group of six products: it moves from one match, HC107, to two, HC107 and Stryker. The public result count after a real correction is unknown.

03

Keep an unanswered question visible.

What I observed
Amstar highlights and specifications give different length and height values. Durastar sections disagree on positioning angle, and AllCare has conflicting width and maximum-height wording.
Why it matters
Someone comparing equipment or planning space needs to know which value applies to the exact configuration.
What I would do
I would retain both values, their source sections and a question for the product owner. A populated field or higher completeness score would not resolve the conflict.
How I would check
Check the repeated values against an approved model-specific source. Until the conflict is resolved, leave the affected comparison field marked Not confirmed.
