How the evidence was collected

A focused sample.
A visible boundary.

The review moves from the wider website into Hospital Beds & Stretchers, then follows selected products through filtering, comparison, and the next action.

36 screenshots appear in this register, including 4 dated follow-up captures. The project retains 39 original screenshots plus 4 follow-up captures, including additional views used by individual findings.

Why these six products?

Vantage, Stryker S3 With Scale, Amstar, Durastar, HomeCare, and AllCare cover a simulation bed, a reconditioned bed, two stretcher types, a configurable homecare bed, and a low bed. Their pages expose different specifications, conditions, and pricing states.

This is a curated sample, not a random or representative sample of the full catalog. Conflicting specifications remain open for confirmation.

What was inspected?

Nine website pages, three category routes, six product pages, a capacity-filter state, and a two-product comparison state. The review covered navigation, product content, existing filters, comparison, mobile presentation, and sampled metadata.

The original desktop and mobile inspections used 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 CSS viewports. A screenshot’s encoded dimensions may differ from its browser viewport. The catalog follow-up uses focused card captures; its separate record preserves the viewport and card bounds for each image.

How should the dates be read?

The 20 original sample observation dates are September 9, 2026 in America/Detroit, with day precision. Individual times for those original screenshots were not recorded.

Product records preserve a separate reviewedAt value in UTC. That value is not the exact screenshot time. This register was assembled September 10 from retained evidence; assembly alone does not imply a fresh website visit.

The September 10 catalog follow-up is separate: each new image records its own observation date and exact capture time in America/Detroit and UTC. It adds evidence to two existing samples without changing their September 9 dates or the other sample records.

What does this review leave open?

Backend configuration, internal search and conversion analytics, manufacturer approval, and business priorities require confirmation. No account authentication or form, quote, or order submission was performed.

Category counts can overlap. Missing fields in a collected sample do not prove missing data in DiaMedical’s systems. Proposed metadata, aliases, summaries, and other local enrichment should remain separate from captured source facts.
