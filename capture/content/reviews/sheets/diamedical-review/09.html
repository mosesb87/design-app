Fourteen products meet this spec.
Eleven can be found.

“I need a reconditioned bed rated for 500 lb. Which ones, and what will they cost?”

I carried that one purchase through DiaMedical’s website. Fourteen products in Hospital Beds & Stretchers state 500 lb or more and are listed as reconditioned; the filters as they stand today reach eleven of them, and not one of the fourteen shows a price. Every strength and every opportunity in this review came out of following that single question to its end.

What already works

Build on a useful foundation.

DiaMedical already connects equipment, educational guidance, and procurement support. I would keep these strengths visible as the experience develops.

01Keep the education-led product storytelling+

The homepage uses real simulation and equipment imagery for Vantage beds, loaded crash carts, diagnostic equipment, manikins and lab furnishings. At the tested mobile width, the Vantage campaign becomes a full-width lead card, followed by smaller product cards.

What I would preserve. Preserve the strong product imagery and direct product/category links when refining the shopping experience.

Inspect the evidence
02Educational guidance already connects needs to equipment+

The Sim Lab Startup Guide explains equipment groups such as patient-care manikins, bed suites, headwalls and infusion pumps, and supplies product links. The Virtual Learning Center groups product knowledge, simulation innovation and educational tips.

What I would preserve. Use this existing expertise as the foundation for task-based guidance and connect relevant guide sections to product decisions.

Inspect the evidence
03Procurement and support routes are already present+

The primary navigation exposes catalog requests and White Glove Delivery & Installation. The resources page offers seven labeled PDF catalog links plus a printed-catalog request. The delivery page lists assembly, headwalls, privacy systems, inventory/testing and scheduling, with a service-request link. Phone, email and chat support are visibly available.

What I would preserve. Carry these practical procurement aids into category and product decision points, including the distinction between buying equipment and arranging installation.

Inspect the evidence
04Useful discovery tools are already present+

The parent category displays 30 products, Hospital Beds 21 and Stretchers 12. Hospital Beds exposes brand, condition, positions and capacity facets. Selecting 500 lbs returns 6 products and exposes Clear All with a filter-specific URL.

What I would preserve. Preserve category links, sort, capacity/condition filters, result count and reset behavior while refining their labels and visual hierarchy.

Inspect the evidence
05Keep the practical depth in product pages+

Vantage has Description, Specifications, Media & PDFs and Warranty tabs, with capacity, patient surface and dimensions in Specifications. Stryker S3 includes brochure/specification links. HomeCare exposes color, side rail, mattress and size selections. The inspected quote-only PDPs explicitly describe pricing and longer lead times.

What I would preserve. Retain this depth, add a concise facts strip near the product title, and preserve downloadable source links and configuration requirements.
