  1. 01

    Comparison pricing conflicts with the quote journey

    Proposed stage
    First
    Owner
    Catalog + eCommerce
    Effort
    Medium

    Confirm which products have a listed price, a price range, or require a quote. Use that approved status consistently in cards, comparison, quick view, and product details. Document the expected label and action on each surface. A zero value alone should not determine whether a product requires a quote.

    Evidence, validation & acceptance

    Observed on the public site

    The Vantage and Stryker S3 With Scale comparison shows $0.00 and Add To Cart for both. Their full product pages show Call for Price and Add To Quote. The comparison CTA opened a quick view with Add To Quote.

    Observed: 2026-09-09 America/Detroit

    Open source page Inspect captured evidence

    How to validate

    Repeat the comparison with the two confirmed quote products and a separately checked priced product. Quote labels and actions should agree across the pages, while the priced product retains its correct price and configuration behavior. Confirm the source pricing status with the catalog and web teams before implementation.

    Acceptance criteria

    • No quote-only product displays $0.00 or an Add To Cart label; priced products retain verified prices and configuration context.
  2. 02

    Resolve contradictory specifications at the source

    Proposed stage
    First
    Owner
    Catalog + eCommerce
    Effort
    Medium

    Record both values, their source sections, the exact product/configuration, and a question for the product owner. Resolve the difference against an approved source, then use the approved value consistently. Until then, comparisons should display Not confirmed.

    Evidence, validation & acceptance

    Observed on the public site

    Amstar highlights list 84.5″ length and 23″–35″ height, while Specifications list 83″ length and 21″–33.5″ height. Durastar highlights say ±16° Trendelenburg while Description says 18° maximum. AllCare's copy offers height "up to 30″" while its specifications give the Hi-Lo setting as 3.6″–25″, and its expandable width is punctuated "35″, 39, – 42″".

    Observed: 2026-09-09 America/Detroit

    Open source page Inspect captured evidence

    How to validate

    Check each repeated measurement against the approved source and configuration. Any remaining differences should be explained or left open. A completeness score cannot establish that a measurement is correct, even when every field is populated.

    Acceptance criteria

    • All repeated measurements agree with an approved source or explain configuration differences. Unresolved dimensions remain Not confirmed in the prototype.
  3. 03

    Name the mobile navigation button for assistive technology

    Proposed stage
    First
    Owner
    Web development
    Effort
    Small

    Give the control a meaningful accessible name and include its state, keyboard activation, close behavior, and focus return in a focused development ticket.

    Evidence, validation & acceptance

    Observed on the public site

    At 390×844 the visible hamburger control appears in the accessibility tree as an unnamed button. The control (.header-sidebar-toggle) contains an SVG icon and carries no aria-label, no title and no visible text. Expanding it opens the navigation drawer.

    Observed: 2026-09-09 America/Detroit

    Open source page Inspect captured evidence

    How to validate

    Confirm that assistive technology announces the name and state, and that keyboard users can open and close the drawer with focus returning to the control. This checks the observed issue; a full accessibility audit would cover more of the site.

    Acceptance criteria

    • The button is announced with a meaningful name.
    • The expanded state follows the visible drawer.
    • Keyboard activation, Escape/close and return of focus are tested with the drawer.

Product detail to comparison

Use the same cohort and reporting window. Check selection persistence and product identifiers; comparison use alone does not prove a better buying decision.

03

Comparison to quote action

Unique eligible sessions that click a quote action after a comparison, divided by unique eligible sessions that view a comparison containing at least two products.

Record product identifiers and the page of origin. A quote click indicates intent; it does not establish a submitted lead.
