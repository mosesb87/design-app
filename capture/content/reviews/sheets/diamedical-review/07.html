Before · what was observed

In the saved comparison, Vantage and Stryker show $0.00 and Add To Cart. Their product pages instead say Call for Price and Add To Quote.

Why this matters

A buyer comparing equipment could mistake a quote-only item for an item with a published price. The labels disagree; customer confusion is a risk to test, not a measured outcome.

What the proposed change does

The local rule assigns one quote-required state to these two products. The price label and action then follow that state across the four demonstrated views.

After · what to test

Apply the local rule below to test the proposal. The expected result is a consistent quote label and action across four views; the current baseline passes 1 of 3 checks.
