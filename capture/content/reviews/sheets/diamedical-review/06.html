Strength to preserve

Keep the education-led product storytelling

01 What I observed

The homepage uses real simulation and equipment imagery for Vantage beds, loaded crash carts, diagnostic equipment, manikins and lab furnishings. At the tested mobile width, the Vantage campaign becomes a full-width lead card, followed by smaller product cards.

The judgment · a strength to keep

The imagery introduces the storefront.

A homepage should help visitors understand what a business offers and find a useful starting point. The recorded DiaMedical homepage does this through recognizable equipment, teaching environments, and direct shopping actions. A visitor can see the kind of products being offered before opening an individual product page. That is why this finding is a strength.

The recommendation here is to retain that equipment context and its working links. Whether a visitor who does not know a product name can find a planning route is a different question. That is covered in the related opportunity, with its own evidence and working proposal.
