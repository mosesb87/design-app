What I would not do

Boundary

Not touch strategy or brand direction

Those stay with channel owners. My job is execution, coordination, and honest reporting on what shipped.

Boundary

Not try to be a data scientist

The JD is clear that this isn't a modeling role. AI helps me summarize and spot anomalies; humans validate; big questions escalate.

Boundary

Not turn the site into a science project

Every ticket has a business reason and an owner. If it doesn't, it doesn't get pulled.
