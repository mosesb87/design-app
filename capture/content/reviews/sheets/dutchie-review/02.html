Where it sits in the launch system

A launch board is supposed to be the single source of truth, with clear ownership of what must be true before GA. This gate is that list, made checkable: each surface gets an owner and a state, the launch can't be called GA while any required surface is red, and the same run on day 30 catches the article that never flipped from “Coming soon.”
