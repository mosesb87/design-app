Why a card and not an email

Every field on it is a check

Dates come from C10, the store list from C12, the price from C4/C5, the limit from C1, the disclaimer slot from C16. If the deal fails a check, the card prints the failure at the top instead of the promo — which is the difference between a store getting a shelf talker and a store getting a phone call on Tuesday morning.
