# Company capture — Blueroot Health

## What it is
Blueroot Health (legal entity Vital Nutrients Holdings, Inc. d/b/a Blueroot Health; Blueroot Health™) is a Middletown, Connecticut consumer-health company that owns a portfolio of supplement brands and sells them direct-to-consumer on separate Shopify stores, to practitioners, and through retail/wholesale channels. Its own boilerplate: "Blueroot Health is a consumer health company growing a diverse portfolio of brands that fuel lasting happiness and health for people and the planet. The company's industry-leading brands – including Vital Nutrients®, Bariatric Fusion®, Fairhaven Health®, and Unjury® – offer a suite of clean, innovative, and clinically relevant nutritional solutions trusted by healthcare practitioners, patients, and consumers worldwide." (GlobeNewswire, Feb 10 2026)

- **HQ / ship-from:** 45 Kenneth Dooley Drive, Middletown, CT 06457 — the same address appears in the footer of all four storefronts (captures/fh_home.html, bf_home.html, vn_home.html, unjury_home.html) and on CB Insights.
- **Size:** ~113 employees per the brief (LinkedIn figure; not independently verified — CB Insights and the press releases do not publish a headcount).
- **Founded:** Vital Nutrients established 2000 in Middletown (citybiz Q&A); Blueroot Health as the holding company listed as founded 2018 (CB Insights).
- **Ownership / investors:** North Castle Partners ("investment partner since 2020"); Balance Point Capital announced an investment in Vital Nutrients Holdings, Inc. d/b/a Blueroot Health (PR Newswire, 2022).
- **Leadership:** Jay Schwartz, CEO (appointed Feb 10, 2026, succeeding founder Jane Pemberton; ex-Iovate Health Sciences, Pfizer Consumer Healthcare). Erin Anthony, CMO. Domenic DiSiena, Head of eCommerce (ZoomInfo). See posting.md for sources.
