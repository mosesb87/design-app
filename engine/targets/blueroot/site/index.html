    Fix 1 · finding 1

    Collection health monitor

    The Monday check: every collection the footer links to, with the count Shopify reports in collections.json and what the storefront actually renders. A footer link to zero buyable products is flagged. Below it, the full list of 42 published collections against a storefront of 32 products.

    Rule: unpublish from the Online Store channel when the storefront count is 0 (the collection leaves the sitemap); repoint the footer link when a label promises products the collection no longer holds. Reported counts above 32 mean the collection still contains unpublished legacy items.

      Fix 3 · finding 3

      Promo consistency check

      The five badged products across every place a price appears. Today the badge promises 30% off "select sizes" on single-variant products while the card, the PDP, the JSON-LD and the subscription widget all show full price. Switch to compare-at pricing and every column agrees — and Google's Merchant Center sees the same price the cart charges.

      ProductVariantsBadgeCardPDPOffer.priceSubscribe (10%)Agree?

      Launch check, in order: badge copy → card price → PDP price → JSON-LD → subscription price → cart total, on one badged PDP before go-live.
