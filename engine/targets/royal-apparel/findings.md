# Royal Apparel — role-specific deep findings

Target: https://www.royalapparel.net (custom "WAM"/liveb2b CGI platform). Role: Digital Marketing & Ecommerce Coordinator (website merchandising, SEO/GEO, GA4/GTM validation, feeds for Google Shopping / Faire / Sage / ASI, email with UTMs). Audited 2026-09-28. Every number below was counted in files under `captures/` (see `captures/index.txt` for URL → file → HTTP status → time).

Fetch tally: 39 GET requests + 17 HEAD requests against the target; nothing submitted, no cart or login actions.

## What the site does well (open with these)

**S1. Fresh, on-brand homepage merchandising.** The hero carousel on `/index` has 4 slides whose creative files are dated within the last three weeks — `B2B_S_NS_20260909.jpg` (New Season Styles), `B2B_S_C_20260914.jpg` (Customize), `B2B_S_FP_20260919.jpg` (Screen-print-friendly tees), `B2B_S_USAManu_20260923.jpg` (USA Manufacturer). All 4 slide links (`/Category/New/`, `/customize`, `/Category/Unisex/#filterItem%20Type=416`, `/usa-made`) resolve to live pages, the carousel is ARIA-labelled (`aria-roledescription="carousel"`, live region "Slide 1 of 4"), and the four category tiles (Women / Men / Heavyweight / Vintage Washed) all carry descriptive alt text. Proof: `captures/home-marketing-p.html`.

**S2. Unusually deep product data and imagery.** Style 5051 ships 28 colors × 9 sizes (XS–5X) and 107 alt-view images in its inline `prodJSON`, and the rendered image alts are specific — e.g. `alt="Unisex Short Sleeve Tee BLACK Back"`. Across the 19 live sampled PDPs the product description averages ~73 words of spec copy (fabric weight, seams, label type, country of origin). Product URLs are self-healing: `/product/5051/anything.html`, `/product/5051/` and the CGI `catalog_product.p?...product=5051` all 301 to `/product/5051/Unisex-Short-Sleeve-Tee.html`. Proof: `captures/product-5051.html`, `captures/product-5051-prodJSON.json`, `captures/pdp-sample-analysis.json`, `captures/index.txt` (HEAD rows).

**S3. Clear B2B commercial terms and a search that respects style numbers.** Wholesale prices are public ($6.98 for 5051, $6.84 for 5001W). `/shipping` states the rule precisely (free freight on online orders ≥ $350 per warehouse, $9.95 handling, in-stock only, updated Oct 30 2025); `/customer-service` gives phone 866.769.2517, fax, HQ address (91 Cabot Court, Hauppauge NY 11788), hours and the returns process (RA number, 20% restocking, 10-day window). Site search for "5051" returns 9 results with 5051 in position 1. Proof: `captures/shipping.html`, `captures/customer-service.html`, `captures/search-5051.html`.

## Findings

### F-01 — Homepage is a meta-refresh stub; four hostname/protocol variants and zero canonicals
- **id:** F-01
- **Observed:** `https://www.royalapparel.net/` returns a 515-byte static file (Last-Modified 21 May 2026) containing only `<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="0; url=…/cgi-bin/liveb2b/wam_tmpl/marketing.p?site=B2B&layout=Baseb2b&page=homepage">` plus `document.location.replace(...)`, a self-referencing `<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.royalapparel.net/">`, no `<title>` and no meta description. That CGI URL then 301s to `/index`, which holds the real homepage (title, description, `og:url=https://www.royalapparel.net/index`) but **no canonical**. So the crawlable chain is `/` (200, stub, canonical → itself) → meta refresh → `marketing.p` (301) → `/index` (200, no canonical): the page that ranks and the page that has the content disagree on which URL is the homepage. Separately, `http://royalapparel.net/`, `http://www.royalapparel.net/`, `https://royalapparel.net/` and `https://www.royalapparel.net/` all answer **200 OK** with byte-identical bodies — there is no http→https or non-www→www 301 (an HSTS header is sent, which browsers only honour after a first HTTPS visit).
- **Count / sample:** 4 of 4 host/protocol variants return 200 (0 redirect); 0 of 24 HTML pages fetched carry a canonical tag (`/index`, `/Category/Men/`, `/Category/Face-Cover-Accessories/`, 20 PDPs, search page) — the only canonical on the site is on the redirect stub. Lower-case `/category/men/` also returns 200 (same content), while `/Category/Men` without the slash returns 404.
- **Why it matters for this role:** the posting owns technical SEO and GEO/AI visibility; with no canonicals and four live hostnames, link equity and AI-crawler citations split across duplicates and the homepage's own signals point at an empty stub.
- **Fix (platform-agnostic, web-server level):** (1) In the Apache/nginx vhost, 301 `http://*` → `https://www.royalapparel.net` and `https://royalapparel.net` → `https://www.royalapparel.net`, and 301 `/` → `/index` (or, better, serve the homepage template at `/` and 301 `/index` → `/`). (2) Delete the meta-refresh stub. (3) Add a self-referencing canonical to the WAM `layout` template head: `<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.royalapparel.net{pretty-path}">`, emitted on `/index`, every `/Category/…/` page and every `/product/{style}/{slug}.html`. (4) Make `/category/men/` (lower-case) 301 to `/Category/Men/` and `/Category/Men` (no slash) 301 to the slashed form.
- **Proof:** `captures/home-root.html`, `captures/home-root-nonwww.html`, `captures/redirect-chains.txt`, `captures/home-marketing-p.html` (the `/index` capture), `captures/index.txt` (HEAD rows for the four variants, `/category/men/`, `/Category/Men`).

### F-02 — Sitemap: 38% redirecting CGI URLs, 16 cart/login/checkout pages, and dead products served as 200
- **id:** F-02
- **Observed:** `https://royalapparel.net/sitemap.xml` (the one robots.txt points to; note the non-www host) lists 1,063 URLs in a single `<urlset>`. 408 are `…/cgi-bin/liveb2b//wam_tmpl/catalog_browse.p?site=B2B&layout=Baseb2b&page=catalog_browse&browsePath=NNN`; every one tested 301s to a pretty URL (browsePath=464 → `/Accessory-Type/Beanies/`, browsepath=205 → `/Category/Men/`), and the pretty `/Category/…` targets are not in the sitemap at all. 36 more are other CGI templates including `cart.p`, `abandoned_carts.p`, `web_checkout.p` (×2), `cobrand_checkout.p`, `login.p`, `reset_password.p`, `pay_invoice.p`, `wish_list.p`, `customer_registration.p`, `order_results.p`, `user_search.p`, `customer_search.p`, plus `marketing.p?page=test-seo`, `/404` and `/Register`. The 248 `/product/` URLs have no `<lastmod>`; the 403 `<lastmod>` values that do exist top out at **2024-07-23**. Of 20 sampled product URLs, `/product/15250006/USA-Team-Beanie.html` returns HTTP 200 with the body "Product Not Found" (a soft 404 with no `prodJSON`), and two more (20055 Unisex Triblend V-Neck, 32112 Women's eco Triblend Scoop Neck) carry `"itemStatus":"obsolete"` in their `prodJSON` while still being listed and indexable.
- **Count / sample:** 408 of 1,063 sitemap URLs (38.4%) are redirecting CGI category URLs; 16 are transactional/account pages; 0 of 248 product URLs have lastmod; 3 of 20 sampled product URLs (15%) are dead or obsolete products still returning 200.
- **Why it matters for this role:** the posting's first duty is "deactivate discontinued items" and keep the catalog crawlable — a sitemap that spends 40% of its entries on redirects and lists checkout/login pages wastes crawl budget and tells Google the product feed is unmaintained.
- **Fix:** regenerate the sitemap from the catalog database nightly: only `https://www.royalapparel.net/…` pretty URLs (`/index`, `/Category/{slug}/`, `/Accessory-Type/{slug}/`, `/product/{style}/{slug}.html`, the landing pages), with a real `<lastmod>` per product from the item's last-edit timestamp; exclude any `itemStatus` of `obsolete`, any style whose page renders "Product Not Found", and every CGI template. Serve the file at `https://www.royalapparel.net/sitemap.xml` and update the `Sitemap:` line in robots.txt to the www host. Make "Product Not Found" return HTTP 404 (or 410 for retired styles) and 301 obsolete styles to their replacement style where one exists (the PDP already knows its "Related Styles").
- **Proof:** `captures/sitemap.xml`, `captures/sitemap-urls.txt`, `captures/robots.txt`, `captures/product-15250006.html`, `captures/product-20055.html`, `captures/product-32112.html`, `captures/pdp-sample-analysis.json`, `captures/index.txt` (301 rows for browsePath=464 and browsepath=205).

### F-03 — PDP structured data is split, blank on availability, and carries a hardcoded 5★/326 rating on every product
- **id:** F-03
- **Observed:** each PDP emits three `application/ld+json` blocks: (a) a `Product` with `sku`, `image`, an HTML-laden `description` and one `Offer` whose `"url":""`, `"priceValidUntil":""` and `"availability":""` are empty and which has no `brand`, `gtin` or `mpn`; (b) a `Corporation` block with `"url":""` and `"logo":""`; (c) a **second** `Product` block with `brand: "Royal Apparel"` and `aggregateRating {ratingValue 5, ratingCount 326}` but no offers. The rating is identical on every product sampled — from a $6.44 beanie to a $33.45 hoody. No PDP has `BreadcrumbList` or `og:image`. Feed-relevant attributes that Google Shopping / Faire need are only partly exposed: color (28 values) and size (9) exist in the inline `prodJSON`, but every color's `hexColor` is the placeholder `"FFFFFF"`, `availSizes` is empty for all 28 colors and `show_stock` is `false` on all 9 sizes; brand, GTIN/UPC and per-variant availability are not present anywhere in the HTML.
- **Count / sample:** 19 of 19 live sampled PDPs have 2 separate `Product` blocks; 19/19 `Offer.availability` = ""; 19/19 identical `aggregateRating` 5/326; 0/20 `BreadcrumbList`; 0/20 `og:image`; 0/20 GTIN or MPN; 150 of 150 colors across the sample have `hexColor` "FFFFFF"; 0/20 PDPs include a video (`"video":""`).
- **Why it matters for this role:** the posting asks for feed optimization for paid search and marketplaces, and Google's product rich results require a single Product with brand, offers.availability and an authentic rating — a sitewide fabricated 326-review count is the kind of thing that gets Merchant Center listings disapproved or a manual action.
- **Fix:** in the `catalog_product` template, output one `Product` object: `name`, `sku`/`mpn` (= style code), `brand {"@type":"Brand","name":"Royal Apparel"}` (or "Basic Supply Co." when `millCode` = BSC), `image` array from `altView`, plain-text `description`, `offers` as an `AggregateOffer` with `lowPrice`/`highPrice` from `regLowPrice`/`regHighPrice`, `priceCurrency USD`, `url` = the pretty URL and `availability` derived from the same `availQty` the JS uses (`https://schema.org/InStock` / `OutOfStock` / `BackOrder`); drop the `aggregateRating` unless it is fed from real review data; add `BreadcrumbList` (Home › Category › Product) and `og:image` = `imageLg`. Expose GTIN/UPC per SKU in the item feed export even if not on the page. Fill `hexColor` from the swatch palette so feeds and filters can carry a real color value.
- **Proof:** `captures/product-5051.html`, `captures/product-5001W.html`, `captures/product-5051-prodJSON.json`, `captures/pdp-sample-analysis.json` (fields `ld_product_blocks`, `offer_avail`, `rating`, `hex_ffffff`, `video`), `captures/analyze_pdps.py`.

### F-04 — GA4/GTM dataLayer: array-wrapped page pushes, blank impression names, UA/GA4 hybrid events
- **id:** F-04
- **Observed (quoted from source, nothing executed):** GTM `GTM-TG38JDV` loads on every page and `wamInitObj` declares `'gaID':'309090160', 'gtmID':'GTM-TG38JDV', 'enhancedEcomm':true`. The page-level push on every template is `dataLayer.push([{'site':'B2B','pageID':'catalog_product','pageType':'catalog_product','loggedIn':false,'language':'en'}])` — the object is wrapped in an **array**, so `pageType`/`pageID` are not readable as plain Data Layer Variables. On `/Category/Men/` the listing push is `impressionList = {"impressions":[{"item_name":"","item_id":"B4001","price":"$4.93","item_brand":"","item_category":"Short Sleeve Tees","list":"Shop-in-Shop","position":1}, …]}` — `item_name` and `item_brand` are empty strings for all 21 items and `price` is a formatted string. The PDP pushes `{"event":"view_item","ecommerce":{"items":[{"item_name":"UNISEX SHORT SLEEVE TEE","item_id":"5051","value":"6.98","item_category":"Unisex"}]}}` — `value` is used at item level instead of `price`, there is no `currency`, `item_brand`, `item_variant` or `quantity`; the related-products push uses the Universal Analytics shape `{"ecommerce":{"currencyCode":"USD","impressions":[…]}}` with GA4 item keys and no `event`, so GA4 never receives a `view_item_list`. In `/js/catalog_product.js` the `add_to_cart` push is written twice, once with `'currencyCode': opts.currency` and once with `'currency': opts.currency` (one of them is wrong for GA4).
- **Count / sample:** 21 of 21 category impressions have blank `item_name` and `item_brand`; 20 of 20 PDP `view_item` pushes lack `currency` and `item_brand`; 1 of 1 page-level push per template is array-wrapped (seen on `/index`, category, PDP, search); 0 `view_item_list` / `select_item` events found in any captured page or JS.
- **Why it matters for this role:** the posting explicitly says "validate GA4 and tag-manager tracking" and build reporting dashboards — with these payloads, GA4 item reports show product IDs with no names, revenue-less views, and no list attribution for the merchandising the coordinator is supposed to optimize.
- **Fix:** in the WAM layout template change `dataLayer.push([{…}])` to `dataLayer.push({…})`; on listing pages emit `dataLayer.push({event:'view_item_list', ecommerce:{item_list_id:'category_men', item_list_name:'Men', items:[{item_id, item_name (from description), item_brand (ROY→'Royal Apparel', BSC→'Basic Supply Co.'), item_category, price (number), index}]}})` and a `select_item` on card click; on PDPs send `view_item` with `ecommerce.currency:'USD'`, `ecommerce.value` and item-level `price`, `item_brand`, `item_variant` (color) — and replace the UA-style related-items push with a second `view_item_list` (`item_list_name:'Related'`). Standardise `add_to_cart` on `currency`. Then verify in GTM Preview + GA4 DebugView, and add a Looker Studio page with item_name/price coverage as a data-quality tile.
- **Proof:** `captures/home-marketing-p.html`, `captures/category-men.html`, `captures/product-5051.html`, `captures/product-5001W.html`, `captures/js-catalog_product.js`.

### F-05 — Category grid: 21 of 136 Men's products in HTML, hash pagination, $0.00 tiles, "New" on everything, Men = Unisex
- **id:** F-05
- **Observed:** `/Category/Men/` renders its product grid client-side from an inline `$.wam.browse.init({"browseProd":[…]})` blob through a Handlebars template; the server HTML contains **one** `/product/` link (a mega-menu entry) and the page says `Page 1 of 7 for 136 Products` with `prodPerPage: 21`. Pages 2–7 are reached by `location.hash='page=2'; location.reload()` — there are no `<a href="?page=2">` links and no `rel=next/prev`, so 115 of the 136 products are not linked from any crawlable URL on the category. Inside the 21 loaded products: 3 (5151GD, 19151GD, 20551GD — custom garment-dye programs) show `regPriceDisp "$0.00"` with no color swatches; all 21 have `"isNew": true` (the "New" badge markup is commented out in the template, so the flag is meaningless); position 1 is `B4001 "Basic Supply Co. Unisex Short Sleeve 5.5 Blend Jersey Tee"` (`"mill":"BSC"`, $4.93) — an imported item leading the Men's grid of a brand whose hero slide says "USA Manufacturer"; the default sort is `sortby=manual`. The left-nav counts show **Men (136)** and **Unisex (136)** — the same set under two URLs. The "Accessories" category still lives at the pandemic-era slug `/Category/Face-Cover-Accessories/` (21 of its 38 items loaded; 4 on sale). The search results for "5051" interleave Basic Supply Co. B1001 ($3.99) and B1015 ($3.55) in positions 2–3 ahead of the 5051 family (5051ORG, 5051T, 5051USA).
- **Count / sample:** 21 of 136 Men's products (15%) present in HTML; 3 of 21 tiles priced $0.00; 21 of 21 flagged new; 1 of 21 is an import in position 1; 2 categories (Men, Unisex) with identical 136 counts; 90 of 359 `<img>` tags on the category page have no alt.
- **Why it matters for this role:** website merchandising — "tagged product groups/categories for campaigns", sort order, deactivating discontinued items — is the core of the posting, and the data shows the levers (new flags, manual sort, price display, category membership) are not being curated.
- **Fix:** (1) Server-render the first page of cards (name, price, pretty link) inside the grid `<div>` and add real pagination links `?page=2…7` with a canonical to page 1 (or `rel=next/prev`), keeping the hash filters for UX. (2) Give custom-dye programs a "Request pricing" tile state instead of `$0.00`, or exclude them from price-sorted lists. (3) Reset `isNew` to a 60-day window from `streetDate` so the New badge (and the `/Category/New/` collection, 81 items) means something. (4) Decide the Men vs Unisex taxonomy: canonicalise `/Category/Men/` to `/Category/Unisex/` or curate a distinct Men's assortment. (5) Rename the slug `/Category/Face-Cover-Accessories/` → `/Category/Accessories/` with a 301. (6) Pin Royal-made styles above Basic Supply Co. imports in "manual" sort on USA-positioned categories and in style-number search (boost exact style-family matches).
- **Proof:** `captures/category-men.html`, `captures/category-men-browse-data.json`, `captures/category-face-cover-accessories.html`, `captures/search-5051.html`.

### F-06 — Size charts are images with the wrong alt text; no product video; "News" page has no news; sitewide footer link 404s
- **id:** F-06
- **Observed:** the only size guidance (`/product-specifications`, linked as "Size Charts" in every footer and from the PDP "Specs" tab) is 61 words of text plus 18 images and **zero** HTML tables or PDFs; 13 of the 15 chart images carry `alt="Pick your color"` (copied from a color page), the other 2 say "Size Chart". The PDP itself lists sizes (XS–5X) but no measurements. No sampled PDP has a video (`"video":""`, `"videoTh":""` in all 19 live `prodJSON`s) even though the posting asks the coordinator to "edit/add product photography/videos". The footer "News" link goes to `/in-the-news`, which has a 47-word intro and no articles or dates; the newest content date anywhere in the sitemap is 2024-07-23 (three company press stories from Oct 2025–Jul 2026 — West Coast DC, second Hauppauge plant, Basic Supply Co. — appear only on trade sites). The footer "Eco-Certifications" link (`/eco-certifications`) returns **404** on every page, next to a stray `--->` from a broken HTML comment. Image file names are opaque timestamps (`50511Front_072226104439.png`; 38 of 38 large images across the sample match `<code>_<timestamp>.png`), and the homepage has no `<h1>`.
- **Count / sample:** 18 chart images, 0 tables; 13/15 chart alts = "Pick your color"; 0/19 PDPs with video; 0 dated posts on `/in-the-news`; 1 sitewide footer link → 404; 38/38 image filenames keyword-free.
- **Why it matters for this role:** these are exactly the content, accessibility and "GEO/AI-visibility" items the posting lists — an AI answer engine cannot read a size chart that is a JPEG labelled "Pick your color", and a news page with no news undercuts the "USA manufacturer expanding" story the company is paying trade press to tell.
- **Fix:** rebuild `/product-specifications` as HTML `<table>`s (one per fit: Women's, Men's/Unisex, Youth, Toddler, Infant, Pants) with a `<caption>` and inch/cm units, and surface the matching table in the PDP "Specs" tab keyed on `sizeGroup`; fix the 13 alts. Publish the three 2025–26 press stories on `/in-the-news` with dates and `NewsArticle` schema, then keep a monthly cadence (new-style drops from the `/Category/New/` list). Point the footer link at a real `/eco-certifications` page (GOTS/OEKO-TEX/RPET claims already live in copy) or remove it. Shoot short colorway/fit clips for the top 20 styles and set `video`/`videoTh` in the catalog so the existing PDP template can play them. Rename new image uploads to `royal-apparel-5051-unisex-short-sleeve-tee-black-front.png`.
- **Proof:** `captures/product-specifications.html`, `captures/in-the-news.html`, `captures/home-marketing-p.html` (footer + h1 check), `captures/pdp-sample-analysis.json` (`video` field), `captures/index.txt` (HEAD `/eco-certifications` → 404).
