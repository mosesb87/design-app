An independent review of royalapparel.net for the Digital Marketing & Ecommerce Coordinator role. I read the homepage, the sitemap's 1,063 URLs, the Men's category and 20 product pages — 39 page fetches plus 17 header checks, no logins, no carts. The garments are well documented: 28 colors, 9 sizes and 107 photos on style 5051. The data around them is not: no canonical on any page, a sitemap that is 38% redirects, a five-star rating hard-coded on every product, and GA4 events whose product names are empty strings. Six findings, five working fixes.

Why this review

Why Royal Apparel

Two new facilities, a West Coast warehouse and a new import division in twelve months means more styles, two inventory states and a second brand to keep straight in categories, feeds and tracking. That is the job in the posting.

Everything here comes from public pages: served HTML, the sitemap, robots.txt and the inline product JSON the site itself uses to render. Nothing was submitted, carted or logged into. The platform is custom, so every fix is written at the template or web-server level rather than for a particular app.

What ships with this page. A workbook with the 29-check scorecard, the six findings with their evidence files, a merchandising QA sheet for the weekly pass, and the 30/60/90 plan. Download the workbook.

    Fix 1 · finding 1

    One homepage, one hostname

    The chain a crawler walks today, and the one it should walk. Then the two rules that do it and the canonical every template would emit.

    Today

    After

    # Apache (.htaccess or vhost)
RewriteEngine On
# http → https, non-www → www, one hop
RewriteCond %{HTTPS} off [OR]
RewriteCond %{HTTP_HOST} !^www\.royalapparel\.net$ [NC]
RewriteRule ^ https://www.royalapparel.net%{REQUEST_URI} [R=301,L]
# the homepage lives at / ; retire the stub and /index
RewriteRule ^index/?$ / [R=301,L]
# case-fold and slash categories
RewriteRule ^category/(.*)$ /Category/$1 [R=301,L,NC]
RewriteRule ^(Category|Accessory-Type)/([^/]+)$ /$1/$2/ [R=301,L]
    <!-- wam_tmpl/layout: emitted on every page -->
<link rel="canonical"
      href="https://www.royalapparel.net{{ pretty_path }}">
<!-- pretty_path resolves to: -->
/                                      homepage
/Category/Men/                         category
/product/5051/Unisex-Short-Sleeve-Tee.html  product
<!-- never a cgi-bin URL, never a hash -->
