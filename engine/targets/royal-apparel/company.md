# Royal Apparel, Inc. — company brief (public sources, checked 2026-09-28)

**What it is.** A privately owned, vertically integrated manufacturer of USA-made blank apparel — tees, tanks, fleece, kids/infant, activewear, headwear and organic/recycled ("ECO + USA") lines — sold blank to screen printers, embroiderers, promotional-product distributors (ASI/Sage), retailers and brands, plus direct wholesale online at royalapparel.net. Founded 1992 (the site says "Born out of a passion for American fashion… founded in the early 1990s in the heart of Manhattan's Garment District"; the careers page says "Founded in 1992"). HQ and manufacturing: 91 Cabot Court, Hauppauge, NY 11788 (Long Island); tel 866.769.2517.

**Size.** ~100 employees (target brief); the company describes itself as having "the largest collection of USA made premium knitwear in our industry" and "millions of garments available for immediate shipment" (Apparelist, Jan 2026). Catalog on the public site: 248 product URLs in the sitemap; 23 shopping categories (Women 185, Unisex/Men 136, Kids 70, Sustainable 86, Sale 50…).

**Brands / stores.**
- royalapparel.net — the B2B/wholesale store (public wholesale pricing, account sign-up, East Coast + West Coast distribution).
- Basic Supply Co. (basicsupplyco.net) — new imported-blank division launched July 2026, cross-listed inside royalapparel.net (e.g. B4001, B1001, B1015 appear in categories and search).
- California Blanks page (`/california-blanks`) and Union-made line (`/wholesale-union-apparel`) are sub-programs on the same site.
