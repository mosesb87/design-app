## Summary (as the posting frames it)
Royal Apparel seeks a coordinator to support growth across ecommerce, wholesale, and promotional channels. The role involves managing digital marketing initiatives, improving online visibility, and maintaining website content.

## Responsibilities (as close to verbatim as the accessible sources allow)
Website merchandising
- Add/update styles, colors, sizes and inventory; deactivate discontinued items
- Manage tagged product groups/categories for campaigns
- Create coupons; update sliders, banners and popups
- Edit/add product photography and videos

SEO & content
- SEO and content editing across the ecommerce and wholesale platforms; publish across platforms
- Technical SEO, keyword-rank tracking, organic visibility, GEO / AI-visibility

Conversion & analytics
- Product, category and landing-page improvements; review funnel performance
- Validate GA4 and tag-manager tracking; maintain reporting dashboards

Email
- Update contact lists; proof email creative; build campaigns in templates with UTM tracking

Paid media support
- Assist with paid search and social campaigns; optimize shopping feeds; conversion tracking

Marketplaces / promo channels & affiliates
- Add product information for Faire, Sage and ASI
- Review affiliate performance

## Requirements (verbatim where quoted by the mirror)
- "Experience (2-5 Years) in digital marketing, ecommerce, merchandising, or a related role"
- "Familiarity with SEO, paid media support, website content publishing, and analytics"
- Strong attention to detail; effective written communication
- "Experience with ecommerce platforms, CMS tools, email platforms, GA4, and reporting tools is preferred"
- Fashion/apparel experience is a bonus

## Benefits listed
Paid vacation, 401(k), profit sharing, medical/dental/vision, employee discount, year-end bonus
