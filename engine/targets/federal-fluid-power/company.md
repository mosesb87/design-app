# Company capture — Federal Fluid Power, Inc.

## What it is
Federal Fluid Power is a Michigan hydraulic component distributor. Its own words (homepage, captures/homepage.html): "a full service hydraulic component distributor offering application engineering services and design assistance. With more than 35 hydraulic component part lines represented by over 30 different manufacturers, Federal Fluid Power has components in stock to get you up and running and keep you moving." The About/Services page adds assembly of custom hydraulic valve manifolds, repair and troubleshooting, and "design and consultation to fabrication and installation of components or entire power units and systems" (captures/about_ffp-federal-fluid-power-info.html).
