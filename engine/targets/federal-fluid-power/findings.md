# Federal Fluid Power (federalfp.com) — role-specific findings

Role: Full Stack IT Specialist, E-Commerce Developer & Content Editor (Plymouth, MI). Audit date: 2026-09-28. Platform: Magento 2 with Hyvä theme, Amasty Shop By / Product Attachments, Magezon page builder; one install serves federalfp.com and patriothyd.com (same `static/version1789741354`, identical robots.txt). Sample: 19 product pages (2 from the Couplings listing + 17 drawn from sitemap files 001/005/009), 5 listing pages, 3 of 9 sitemap files (54,496 URLs), 8 CMS pages. Every number below is counted from files in `captures/`.

## What the site does well

1. **Part-number search actually works.** `https://federalfp.com/catalogsearch/result/?q=FI-TE-04LLMK-W3-MS` returns exactly one hit, "FI-TE-04LLMk-W3-MS - 6010000456 $22.90 Add to Cart", and the results page is `noindex,follow` with a canonical to `/catalogsearch/result/` — correct handling for a B2B buyer typing a manufacturer code. (captures/search_FI-TE-04LLMK-W3-MS.html)
2. **Product structured data is more complete than most distributors ship.** Every sampled PDP carries `Product` + `Offer` (price, USD, availability), `shippingDetails` (handling 2–5 business days, $9.99 rate, US) and `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`, plus a `BreadcrumbList`; category pages add `BreadcrumbList` and an `OfferCatalog`. 19/19 PDPs. (captures/pdp_fi-te-04llmk-w3-ms-6010000456.html)
3. **The Engineering Toolbox is real content, not filler.** `/engineering-toolbox/` is a 2,570-word in-browser calculator suite (cylinder bore/rod area, force, speed, flow, rod buckling; pumps, HPU, pneumatic, electrical, conversions) with "Shop Cylinders →" links back into the catalog, and the footer also links a Hose Configurator and Product Configurator. (captures/engineering-toolbox.html)

## Findings

### F-01 — 37% of sitemap product URLs have no URL key and are blocked by robots.txt
- **Observed:** `robots.txt` says `Disallow: /catalog/product/view/`, yet `sitemap_005.xml` lists 10,879 URLs and `sitemap_009.xml` 9,363 URLs of the form `https://federalfp.com/catalog/product/view/id/93633/` — products that were imported without a `url_key`. Fetching three of them returns HTTP 200, and each page's own canonical points to another blocked URL, e.g. `https://federalfp.com/catalog/product/view/_ignore_category/1/id/101454/s/804500106/`. Google receives 20k URLs it is told not to crawl.
- **Count:** 20,242 of 54,496 URLs (37.1%) across the 3 sitemap files fetched (0 in 001, 10,879 in 005, 9,363 in 009); 3/3 sampled pages 200 with a robots-blocked canonical.
- **Why it matters:** the job owns ERP → Magento product data, and a missing `url_key` on ~20k SKUs is exactly the kind of import-mapping gap that silently removes products from search.
- **Fix:** in Magento admin, filter the product grid by "URL Key = empty", bulk `Update attributes` (or re-import a CSV with `url_key` = slugified name+SKU), reindex `catalog_url_rewrite`, then regenerate the sitemap (Marketing → Site Map) and verify no `/catalog/product/view/` URLs remain; keep the `Disallow` so the numeric duplicates never index.
- **Proof:** captures/robots.txt, captures/sitemap_005.xml, captures/sitemap_009.xml, captures/pdp_catalog_product_view_id_101454.html, captures/pdp_catalog_product_view_id_97434.html, captures/pdp_catalog_product_view_id_310140.html

### F-02 — Specs live in the title string; attribute values are dirty where they exist
- **Observed:** the "More Information" table on the sampled PDPs is only Product Name / SKU / Weight / Brand (18/19); thread size, port, material and pressure appear only inside the name or description text, e.g. "BV3-04-NPT 1/4\" Three way selector Ball valve w/ 1/4\" NPT Threads.(5,800 PSI)". Where spec attributes do exist (Cylinders, 953 items, `/hydraulics-1/cylinders-1.html`), the layered-nav values are unnormalised: Bore lists both `2.5` and `2.5"`, Stroke both `8` and `8"`, Bore x Stroke both `2 1/2 x 10` and `2 1/2 X 10`, Rod Diameter both `1 1/4"` and `1.25`, Column Load mixes `11520 lbs` with bare `11`, and `Full PSI` / `FULL PSI`. Two values are Excel date serials: Ports (NPTF) = `44628` and Rod Diameter = `44624` — the classic sign of a fraction like `3/8` auto-converted to a date in a spreadsheet before import. Weights are round defaults: a $3.40 DIN stud coupling weighs "10.000000", a 3/8" brass push-on fitting "5.000000".
- **Count:** 18/19 PDPs with only the 4 default attributes; 0/19 with a thread/port/material/pressure attribute; 3/19 with no weight. Cylinders filters: Bore 6 of 17 values are inch-mark twins, Stroke 4 of 17, Bore x Stroke 72 of 142 values are x/X case twins, Rod Diameter 4 fraction/decimal twins + 1 date serial, Ports (NPTF) 1 value and it is a date serial.
- **Why it matters:** "keep product/inventory data accurate" and "build product listings with attributes" is the core of this role, and these are the errors a buyer hits when filtering.
- **Fix:** define attributes (`thread_size`, `port_size`, `material`, `pressure_rating`, `pack_qty`, `mfr_part_number`) in an attribute set per product line, parse them out of existing names with a one-off script, and normalise option values (strip `"`, lowercase `x`, one unit convention) via `Stores → Attributes → Product → Manage Options`; re-export the cylinder CSV with text-formatted columns so `3/8` never becomes `44628` again.
- **Proof:** captures/pdp_sample.csv, captures/category_hydraulics-1_cylinders-1.html, captures/pdp_bv3-04-npt.html, captures/pdp_fi-ge-06llm6x1k-w3-ms-6010000421.html

### F-03 — Price and availability signals contradict each other (ERP-sync symptoms)
- **Observed:** unavailable items replace "Add to Cart" with a "Request Pricing and Availability" button but still print a price on the PDP (`/adapters-fittings/couplings/fi-ge-06llm6x1k-w3-ms-6010000421.html` shows "$3.40" and the RFQ button), while the listing card hides the price; the JSON-LD for the same items still declares `"availability":"https://schema.org/InStock"`. `804500106` (`/catalog/product/view/id/101454/`) shows "$0.00". No PDP shows a stock quantity or in-stock/out-of-stock text; only the 3 Patriot-brand valves show "Usually Ships Within: 1-3 Days". Whole leaf categories are unpriced: Chairs and Creepers page 1 is 20/20 RFQ. The Couplings price facet offers a "$40,000.00 - $50,000.00" bucket, i.e. at least one coupling is priced above $40k.
- **Count:** 9/19 PDPs are RFQ-only, and 8 of those 9 carry `InStock` schema; 1/19 shows $0.00; Couplings listing p1 3/20 and p2 13/20 RFQ; Chairs and Creepers 20/20 (of 40).
- **Why it matters:** the posting names "oversee Magento 2 integration with the ERP" and "keep inventory data accurate"; these are the public symptoms of stock/price fields not mapping cleanly.
- **Fix:** map ERP quantity and price into `qty`/`is_in_stock`/`price` on every sync run and have the RFQ module key off `is_salable`; render Magento's stock status block (Stores → Configuration → Catalog → Inventory → "Display Out of Stock Products" + stock status on PDP), suppress `Offer` or set `availability` = `OutOfStock`/`BackOrder` when the button is RFQ, and add an admin alert for price = 0 or price > $10,000 on fittings.
- **Proof:** captures/pdp_fi-ge-06llm6x1k-w3-ms-6010000421.html, captures/pdp_catalog_product_view_id_101454.html, captures/category_couplings.html, captures/category_couplings_p2.html, captures/category_chairs-and-creepers.html, captures/pdp_sample.csv

### F-04 — Manufacturer logos stand in for product photos on tens of thousands of SKUs
- **Observed:** 10 of 19 sampled PDPs show a supplier logo as the only product image (`alaindustries.jpg` on five Yuken items, `dnpamerica.jpg`, `stucchi.jpg`, `poclain.jpg`, `Damanlogo.jpg`); the two Stauff couplings share one family drawing (`Stauff_GaugeAndStudConnectors.png`). In the sitemaps the first image is a file shared by ≥100 products for 35,744 of 51,918 products (69%), and one of six logo files for 14,732 (28%); 512 products have no image tag at all. Homepage tiles also carry mismatched alt text (`Fittings.png` alt="Hydraulic Oil", `Metric Fittings.png` alt="PressureGauges").
- **Count:** 14/19 PDPs have one image, 10/19 a logo; sitemap-wide 35,744/51,918 share a generic first image; 512 with none.
- **Why it matters:** the role writes "copy, attributes, images" for the B2B store, and a logo where the part should be is the single most visible content gap for a buyer comparing fittings.
- **Fix:** pull supplier CAD/line-drawing sets (Stauff, Tompkins and Poclain publish them) and import by SKU via `Import → Products` with `base_image`/`small_image`/`thumbnail`, use a per-family drawing as fallback instead of the logo, and set a Magento placeholder (Content → Configuration → Product Image Placeholders) so the 512 image-less SKUs stop rendering blank.
- **Proof:** captures/pdp_sample.csv, captures/sitemap_001.xml, captures/sitemap_005.xml, captures/sitemap_009.xml, captures/pdp_dshg-03-3c4-d24-n1-14-dir-valve-pilot-operated.html, captures/homepage.html

### F-05 — One meta description site-wide, code-only titles and duplicate URLs everywhere
- **Observed:** every sampled PDP and every CMS page fetched carries the identical description "Discover a trusted online destination for all your hydraulic requirements at Federal Fluid Power…"; PDP `<title>` is the bare part code with no brand or site suffix (`<title>BV3-04-NPT</title>`). The URL layer is duplicated three ways: (a) 12 of 17 sitemap-sampled product URLs return 200 but declare a different canonical (sitemap `/hp-10y-4-50-x10-00-yellow-hose-protector.html` → canonical `/hp-10y.html`; `/700018114.html` → canonical `/700018096-2.html`, another SKU's key); (b) all 20 product links on the Couplings listing use category-path URLs whose canonical is the root URL; (c) the homepage links 58 times into an alias tree `/hydraulics-1/…` that serves 200 with a canonical to `/hydraulics/…`, plus one raw `catalog/category/view/s/systems-and-capsules-1/id/14086/` link. 2,361 of 2,484 category URLs in sitemap_001 end in `-1`/`-2`/`-3` (auto-deduplicated keys), and each page emits two `BreadcrumbList` blocks.
- **Count:** 19/19 PDPs + 7/7 CMS pages same description; 11/19 code-only titles; 12/17 sitemap URLs non-canonical; 20/20 listing links non-canonical; 2,361/2,484 (95%) suffixed categories; 58 alias links on the homepage.
- **Why it matters:** "content editor" for a 50k-SKU catalog means templates, not hand edits, and the crawl budget is currently spent on duplicates of the same part.
- **Fix:** set product meta templates in Stores → Configuration → Catalog → Product Fields Auto-Generation (`{{name}} | {{brand}} hydraulic {{category}} | Federal Fluid Power` / description from name + brand + first spec), set "Use Categories Path for Product URLs" = No with "Create Permanent Redirect for URLs if URL Key Changed" = Yes, run a URL-rewrite cleanup so old keys 301 instead of 200, relink the homepage grid to canonical category URLs, and remove the second breadcrumb JSON-LD block from the Hyvä template.
- **Proof:** captures/pdp_sample.csv, captures/pdp_700018114.html, captures/pdp_hp-10y-4-50-x10-00-yellow-hose-protector.html, captures/category_couplings.html, captures/homepage.html, captures/category_hydraulics-1_cylinders-1.html, captures/sitemap_001.xml

### F-06 — Shared-store hygiene: Patriot branding, wrong phone, dead and junk pages
- **Observed:** federalfp.com's footer reads "© 2023 Patriot Hydraulics. All rights reserved" and the header says "Call Us: 734-479-9641", which is Patriot Hydraulics' number (the FFP footer and BBB list 734-455-1722). "Shipping Policy" and "Return Policy" both link to `/terms/`, a 2,320-word legal T&C page, and `/ffp-terms-conditions-of-sale/` is a second copy of it; "Order History" and "Order Tracking" link to `#`. The sitemap's 63 CMS URLs include `/test/`, `/webkul/`, `/webkul-seller/`, `/pictures/`, `/privacy-patriothyd/`, `/enable-cookies/`; `/career-at-federal-fluid-power-inc/` repeats the About text with no openings; the indexed `/brand/patriot-hydraulics` returns HTTP 404 whose body is a clone of the homepage (homepage title, self-canonical, no "not found" message). Every PDP prints "ask about our leads times? click here to chat". Both sampled SKUs exist on patriothyd.com with identical price, copy and image and self-referencing canonicals on each domain.
- **Count:** 2 wrong-brand/phone strings on every page; 2 `#` footer links; 6+ junk CMS URLs of 63; 1 duplicate T&C page; typo on 19/19 PDPs; 2/2 SKUs duplicated across domains.
- **Why it matters:** these are the first things a hiring manager (or a customer) sees, and each is a 10-minute fix inside Content → Blocks/Pages for someone who owns the multi-store setup.
- **Fix:** set store-view-scoped footer/header blocks (copyright, phone) for the FFP store view; create real `/shipping-policy/` and `/returns/` CMS pages and relink the footer; delete or disable the test/webkul/pictures pages and exclude them from the sitemap; assign a proper "no-route" CMS page; fix the typo in the PDP template; and decide one canonical domain per SKU (or noindex the Patriot copies) to stop cross-domain duplication.
- **Proof:** captures/homepage.html, captures/terms_shipping-policy.html, captures/ffp-terms-conditions-of-sale.html, captures/sitemap_009.xml, captures/career.html, captures/brand_patriot-hydraulics.html, captures/patriot_homepage.html, captures/patriot_pdp_fi-te-04llmk-w3-ms-6010000456.html
