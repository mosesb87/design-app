Why Federal Fluid Power

    Working fixes

    Six prototypes, built on your catalog

    Everything below runs on what federalfp.com served on Sep 28, 2026 — the real product names, the real filter values, the real footer. Each one is the Magento change described in the finding, and two of them are the scripts I would run in week one.

    Fix 1 · finding 1

    URL-key generator

    The three keyless products I fetched, plus the sample set, run through the rule Magento's import needs: url_key = slug(name) + "-" + slug(sku), unique, lowercase, no inch marks. The output is the CSV column the bulk import takes; the blocked /catalog/product/view/id/N/ URLs leave the sitemap once the keys exist and the URL rewrites reindex.

    Fix 2 · finding 2
    Option normalizer
    
      
    The Cylinders filter values exactly as the category page serves them, and the same values after one normalization pass: inch marks stripped, x lowercased, fractions restored from Excel date serials, units made consistent. Fractions and decimals meet in one convention (2 1/2 → 2.5, the way Bore already reads). Red = a twin of another value; orange = a date serial.
