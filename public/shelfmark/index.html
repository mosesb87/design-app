    Method

    How a score is built

    Five pillars, weighted by how much they decide a sale. Twenty-nine checks, each scored 2, 1 or 0 against a fixed definition, or marked unverified when a public page could not show it.

    Pillar weights

      pillar % = verified points ÷ (2 × verified checks)
      ShelfMark Score = Σ (weight × pillar %) ÷ Σ weight, over pillars with ≥1 verified check
      coverage = verified checks ÷ 29 · confidence: High ≥ 26, Medium 20–25, Low < 20
      grade: A ≥ 85 · B ≥ 70 · C ≥ 55 · D ≥ 40 · F below
      • Public pages only: homepage, robots.txt, sitemap, one category, two product pages, contact or about. No logins, no purchases.
      • Unverified never counts for or against a store. A client-rendered page or a bot challenge earns a U, not a guess.
      • Structured data (F7) is unverified for 20 of 21 stores because served HTML rarely exposes it to a fetcher; it stays in the rubric for hands-on audits.
      • Scores describe a snapshot on 28 Sep 2026. Stores change; corrections and re-audits are welcome.
