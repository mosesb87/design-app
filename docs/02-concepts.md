# Creative directions and the decision

Three directions were developed independently and scored by a three-judge panel. Each direction had its own metaphor, spatial system, typography, motion language and story structure. The full specifications are in [`concepts/`](concepts/). The scorecards, with every strength, weakness, graft and must-fix, are in [`concepts/judging.md`](concepts/judging.md).

All three start from the research in [`01-research.md`](01-research.md). They share the rules of §10 and §11 of that document: every claim is traceable, there is no invented proof, none of the category clichés, and Sapience's own cream, serif and purple identity is kept.

---

## The core idea behind all three

Sapience AI's own words keep returning to one claim. *The intelligence already exists. It is scattered, and it walks out the door.*

- "The most powerful intelligence in a professional community already lives within its people." (Our values)
- "Insights sit scattered across inboxes, drives, and disconnected tools." (Customers)
- "We sit above your existing systems to organize, connect, and activate relevant knowledge." (LinkedIn)
- "When board members or committee chairs move on, so does the knowledge they carried." (proposed SXSW27 session)

Each direction answers the same question in a different way: **what does it look like when a community's scattered knowledge becomes one answer you can trust, and the trail comes with it?**

---

## Direction A — STRATA: the organization that never forgets

*A survey plate of a professional community. Thirty years of member-built knowledge are laid down as layers. One question is drilled through them as a core, and it comes back as a tray of cited samples. The last compartment is left empty, for the person who was there.*

- **Metaphor:** accrual, sediment and core samples. Sapience already calls its product a "Private Intelligence Core" and itself "the layer".
- **Visual language:** a 19th-century geological survey plate printed in Sapience colours. Bodoni Moda, lithology hatching, neatlines and a cartouche.
- **Motion language:** one continuous "camera take" through a CSS-3D block diagram. Everything falls into place with gravity, and only the answer rises.
- **Signature moment:** the empty fifth compartment in the core tray, with a hairline running to the 2018 gap in the record. The last source is a person.
- **Why it was not chosen:**
  - Drilling cores *out of* members' knowledge is an extraction image, for a buyer whose top AI barrier is privacy.
  - The "continuous take" cannot be built without visible seams between pins.
  - CSS-3D hatched SVG shimmers under scrubbed rotation.
  - The Bodoni survey world would become the brand's costume just as Sapience's own identity refresh lands.

## Direction B — The Annotated Record

*Sapience AI's site typeset as a volume of proceedings. The community's record is printed in charcoal and holds still. Sapience's purple ink moves: it carries what members know out of the margins and into the main text, and every answer arrives with its sources.*

- **Metaphor:** the editorial register associations themselves publish: journals, proceedings, minutes, standards, marginalia and footnotes.
- **Visual language:**
  - Newsreader for display, text and marginalia; Public Sans for the apparatus; IBM Plex Mono for the record-keeping register.
  - A Tufte-style page grid with a real margin column.
  - Purple is used only as ink: carets, leaders, citations and the call to action.
- **Motion language:** *print is set once, ink is live.* Charcoal type never performs, and every continuous movement is purple ink showing where something came from.
- **Signature moment:** an answer is typeset from the margins. Each source rides a leader into the paragraph, changes register from italic margin hand to roman body text as it lands, and leaves its footnote number behind.

## Direction C — Meeting Point: follow the purple line

*Sapience shown as the wayfinding system of an association's annual meeting. Sage is the information desk. The existing systems are rooms that are never moved. One purple route line carries a question to the person who knows, and the route turns out to be the audit trail.*

- **Metaphor:** convention-centre signage, tracks, badges and session boards.
- **Visual language:** transit-grade signage, with a grotesk-led system voice, track colours and pictograms.
- **Signature moment:** *The Alignment.* Twenty-four contradictory signs click round and then snap, in a diagonal wave, to face the same way.
- **Why it was not chosen:**
  - It leaves Sapience's editorial serif register.
  - It adds track colours and a pictogram set that would overlap the brand designer's scope.
  - Its hero promise, "The person who knows →", rests on peer-matching, which is published only as an early-access waitlist in Labs.

## Decision: The Annotated Record, with more motion

**Committed direction: B, The Annotated Record.**

It is the only direction that treats Sapience's identity as something to steward rather than replace. Its honesty is built into the structure: the sources are the design. It is also the one that can reach real polish as plain HTML5 on Google infrastructure within the posting's timeline.

All three judges saw the same weakness. **It could read as too quiet**: "the current site with footnotes", with hairlines that vanish on a projector. The brief asks for a motion-first site, so the decision comes with amendments. Each one strengthens B's own grammar instead of adding a second concept on top of it.

### Amendments: what changes from the B specification

1. **The hero becomes a scene, not a title page.**
   - The display line is short and large: *"Your community already knows."* The H1 also carries the entity and category ("Sapience AI is the collective intelligence platform for professional communities"), for search and disambiguation.
   - The margin beside it is full of illustrative fragments of what a community knows: minutes, session Q&A, a forum thread, a standards draft, a chapter call, and a past chair's reasoning that was never written down.
   - On load, the headline is set line by line. Then purple ink runs from every fragment and converges on one word. Many sources feeding one claim is *collective intelligence* shown as a single image.
2. **The first scroll pulls the camera back.** The hero spread scales down to reveal that it is one page among many: the whole record of a community, laid out on the desk. The pages then drift apart into "inboxes, drives, and disconnected tools", and their leaders stop short in dashed queries ("Qy."). This gives scale, depth and a real hero-to-next-section change without the fragile 3D camera from A.
3. **The scatter settles into the four systems, which then stand still.** The pages settle into AMS, CRM, LMS and content systems. They are never merged. Sapience's layer is drawn as a flat sheet of vellum, not glass, and slides over them. The ink organizes, connects and activates across them. "Nothing underneath has to move."
4. **The signature sequence scales up** (a graft from A).
   - The answer is set at reading size across a full-viewport spread, with 1.5px leaders that hold up on a projector.
   - The **fifth source is a person**, and nothing travels from it. Its leader draws out empty to a dashed double frame: *"Not written down. The reasoning sits with the past chair."*
   - It is labelled *Illustrative · capability to be confirmed by Sapience AI*, because peer-matching is published only as early access.
5. **The trail becomes the audit trail** (a graft from C). After the climax, the five leaders straighten into one vertical trail: question → sources → person → answer → reviewed by the people you permit. It becomes the governance chapter, so the proof follows the climax without a sag in pacing.
6. **Memory gets an image** (a graft from A, without the extraction). "The organization that never forgets" is drawn as the fore-edge of a volume: thirty years of hairline strata. Role tags such as *Chair, 2016–19* fade out while their leaders stay put, so people move on and their notes stay cited. New hairlines arrive each year, because "it gets smarter every year".
7. **The honesty is visible, not hidden:**
   - a persistent concept notice;
   - a **Provenance view** that tags every block as verbatim, adapted, concept, illustrative, industry source, or capability to be confirmed;
   - honest proof slots ("Reports from the field", reserved for approved stories);
   - Affinity and Corporate shown by name only;
   - the flagged figures from the research demoted to footnotes.
8. **It works in the review room.**
   - `?present` turns on presenter mode: number keys jump to chapters, `M` toggles motion, `P` toggles Provenance view.
   - A visible *Motion: On/Off* control ("Your reading. Your control.") reuses NoteBouncer's wording on the site itself.
9. **The motif resolves.** The caret and its leader appear on every page. At the end, the last leader keeps travelling and becomes the underline beneath *Book a conversation*.

### What the direction deliberately does not do
- No 3D camera, WebGL, glass, glow, gradients or particles.
- Nothing is split into letters. Nothing counts up, and no word rotates.
- No invented customers, metrics, testimonials or agent capabilities.
- No motion runs without scroll or interaction, so nothing loops and WCAG 2.2.2 is never triggered.

### Typography (final)
- **Newsreader Variable** (opsz 6–72, wght 200–800) for display and text. It is a deliberate move from Sapience's Baskerville-style headlines into a journal register. Optical sizes let a margin note grow into a display line.
- **Public Sans Variable** for the apparatus: running heads, labels, buttons, figures and the form. It comes from the U.S. Web Design System, so it has a civic, standards-body lineage.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for the record-keeping register: source labels, the trail and the security specifications.
- The body face is a single token (`--font-text`). If Sapience's refresh keeps a sans body, it switches back in one line.

The motion system, set piece by set piece, is in [`03-motion-system.md`](03-motion-system.md).
